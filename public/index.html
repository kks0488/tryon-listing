What should I ask about sizing?

Ask for chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and garment length in your preferred size. Compare with a garment you already own.

What happens to my photo?

No photo database or disk storage is used. Live mode sends images to YouCam. After a completed result is downloaded, the app requests deletion of the task and its inputs/outputs. Pending or interrupted tasks may remain with the provider. Clear this session to remove local previews.
